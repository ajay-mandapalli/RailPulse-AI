Passenger Demand Intelligence System

What is RailPulse?

RailPulse is a railway passenger-demand prediction and decision-support system for Hyderabad MMTS. It helps operators explore route demand, anticipate crowd levels and plan services using historical passenger observations.

Problem

Demand varies by route, departure time and date. Anticipating potentially crowded services is difficult when historical patterns are not easy to interpret.

Solution

RailPulse combines historical passenger data, route information and temporal patterns to forecast passenger counts, classify demand and provide operational recommendations.

Machine learning

The project compares a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) with an Echo State Network (ESN). The saved comparison selects {{ selected_model }}: ESN has lower MAE and RMSE and higher R² on the recorded evaluation.
